Analysis & Rules

Rating Rules

  • Excellent - Attendance 90% or more, Tasks Done 80% or more, Net Points 0 or more.
  • Good - Attendance 75% or more and Tasks Done 50% or more.
  • At Risk - Anything below the Good level.
  • Not Rated - the student has no attendance or no task records yet.
  • Attendance % = Present days / all attendance records. Net Points = Positive - Negative.
