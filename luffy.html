Monkey D. Luffy is the main hero of the Monkey D. Luffy story. He is a cheerful and brave pirate captain. He wants to find the ultimate treasure and become the Pirate King. As a child, he accidentally ate a magical fruit. This fruit turned his body into rubber. He can now stretch his arms and legs. However, he can never swim again. He sails the seas with his loyal crew, the Straw Hat Pirates. He makes strong friends and fights evil foes to protect his ultimate freedom.If you'd like, let me know if you want to learn more about his crew, his Devil Fruit powers, or his bounty.
